# MarraUp Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 28, 2026  
**App:** MarraUp (Android package `com.marraup.app`)

MarraUp helps you explore business direction, assess an operating business, and work through local action plans. This policy describes how the mobile app handles information.

## Summary

- **Local-first:** Business details, assessment answers, scores, action plans, and My Path project data are stored **on your device only** (SQLite).
- **No MarraUp account** in this version: we do not operate a login server or cloud backup of your business data.
- **No sale of personal data** and **no advertising profile** built from your in-app activity in this version.
- **Optional profile photo** uses the system photo picker; images stay on the device unless you share them outside the app yourself.

## Information you provide

You may enter or generate:

- Profile display name, country, and optional profile photo  
- Business name, owner name, sector, and intake details  
- Assessment and discover questionnaire answers  
- Action plan progress, notes, and delivery evidence you add  
- My Path / launch project content (find, blueprint, develop steps)

All of this is **self-reported by you**. MarraUp does not verify, audit, or independently confirm it.

## How we use information

Data is used **only on your device** to:

- Show your progress, scores, plans, and market reference content  
- Remember language and settings  
- Schedule local reminders if you enable notifications  

We do **not** transmit your business or assessment content to MarraUp servers in this version.

## Storage and retention

- Data remains on your phone or tablet until you **delete it in the app** (e.g. remove a business from Profile) or **uninstall the app**.  
- Deletion of a business in Profile is **permanent** for that business’s stored records on the device.  
- Uninstalling the app removes local app data from that device.

## Permissions (Android)

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| **Photos / media (image picker)** | Optional profile picture only, when you choose to add one. |

The app does not require microphone access for core features.

## Third parties

This build does **not** integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs for your business content. Market and sector information shown in the app is **reference material** bundled or loaded for display, not a transfer of your private business answers to those sources.

If you open external links (e.g. market research URLs), those sites have their own policies.

## Children

MarraUp is intended for adults managing or starting a business. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Future features

If a future version adds optional publishing of a business profile to investors or cloud sync, that will require **your explicit choice** and an updated policy before it applies.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

## Contact

For privacy questions about MarraUp, contact the developer using the **support contact on the Google Play listing** for this app, or open an issue in the project repository if a public link is provided there.

---

*This policy applies to the MarraUp native app distributed on Google Play. In-app Privacy (Profile → Settings → Privacy) summarizes the same principles in shorter form.*
