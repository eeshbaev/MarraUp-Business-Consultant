MarraUp Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026
App: MarraUp (Android com.marraup.app)

MarraUp helps you explore business direction, assess an operating business, and work through local action plans. This policy describes how the mobile app handles information.

Summary

  • Local-first: Business details, assessments, scores, plans, and My Path data stay on your device.
  • No MarraUp account in this version — no login server or cloud backup of your business data.
  • No sale of personal data and no ad profile from your in-app activity in this version.
  • Profile photo is optional via the system photo picker; it stays on the device unless you share it elsewhere yourself.

Information you provide

You may enter profile details, business and assessment answers, plan progress, and launch-project notes. All of this is self-reported. MarraUp does not verify or audit it.

How we use information

Data is used only on your device to show progress, scores, plans, and reference content, and to remember settings and local notifications if enabled. We do not send your business or assessment content to MarraUp servers in this version.

Storage and retention

Data remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall. Deleting a business in Profile is permanent on that device.

Permissions (Android)

Permission Why
Photos / media Optional profile picture when you choose to add one.

Third parties

This build does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs for your business content. External links you open are governed by those sites’ policies.

Children

MarraUp is for adults managing or starting a business, not directed at children under 13.

Future features

Optional investor publishing or cloud sync, if added later, will require your explicit choice and an updated policy.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change.

Contact

Use the support contact on the Google Play listing for this app, or the repository issue tracker if linked from the store.
